About Pablo & Evren

About Pablo & Evren

Pablo & Evren met at ZBA (Zentrum für Brückenangebot) in Basel, Switzerland. At first, everything was random — different personalities, different energy, different paths — but somehow they ended up in the same orbit.

Over time, small moments turned into a real friendship. Between studying, school life, and creative chaos, they started building ideas together that slowly turned into music.

People around them started giving them nicknames, inside jokes formed, and their world became its own universe — part serious, part chaotic, part completely unpredictable.

What started as a normal school connection became a creative duo focused on sound, emotion, and building something bigger than just music.

Today, Pablo & Evren combine everything they’ve experienced into their work — turning everyday life into cinematic, high-energy music and performances.
