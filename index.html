The Story

About Pablo & Evren

Pablo and Evren first crossed paths at ZBA — the Zentrum für Brückenangebot in Basel, Switzerland. They were both there to study. Pablo, being honest, clocked Evren from across the room and was convinced he was dealing with a full Talahon, probably very Left. The kind of person you don't approach first.

He was wrong. Once the walls came down, they found something neither expected — a shared obsession with music that goes all the way to the bone. Big ideas. Big sound. Big D energy in everything they made. They called the project HOLY OBESITY, a name that doesn't apologize for taking up space.

Their sound draws from the cinematic ambition of Kanye — that wall-of-sound philosophy, the willingness to go left when everyone expects right. Huge drums, heavy bass, melodies that hit like punches.

What started as two guys pushing through study together became something nobody saw coming. Now they're taking it global — and this World Tour is just the beginning.
